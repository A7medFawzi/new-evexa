Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Subject to Final Corporate Approval

1. About EVEXA and Scope

EVEXA is an artificial intelligence agency and technology studio providing custom AI solutions, specialized enterprise operations platforms, and strategic digital engineering services. This Privacy Policy outlines how EVEXA (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles personal data collected through our corporate website (evexa.tech) and our prospective client inquiry channels.

Scope and Product Separation: This policy applies exclusively to the EVEXA corporate website and its direct lead-qualification and contact processes. It does not apply to:

  • Client Enterprise Deployments: Proprietary client platforms and dedicated enterprise environments, which are governed by independent Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), and Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).
  • Independent Software Products: Commercial software solutions such as Dealplier, which operate under distinct software licenses, terms of service, and product-specific privacy policies.

EVEXA serves as the data controller for personal data processed through this corporate website. Formal registered entity details and corporate registry references are maintained in our corporate records and confirmed upon formal commercial engagement.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to evaluate business inquiries, provide professional responses, and maintain the security of our website.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you submit an inquiry through our B2B Contact Form or correspond with us via email, we collect the details you provide:

  • Full Name: To address you professionally in business correspondence.
  • Company Name: To understand the organization you represent and its operational context.
  • Work Email Address: To reply to your inquiry, deliver proposals, and schedule technical consultations.
  • Country (Optional): To understand your geographic region and applicable timezone.
  • Area of Interest (Optional): Your selected area of focus (such as Custom AI Solutions, Knowledge Search & RAG, Document Intelligence, or Workflow Automation).
  • Project Message: Specific project descriptions, operational challenges, or technical goals you share with our team.

B. Technical and Operational Information Collected Automatically

When you navigate our website, our servers log limited technical data strictly required for reliable page delivery and threat prevention:

  • IP Address: Evaluated in server memory and recorded in technical logs to enforce automated rate limiting (deterring denial-of-service, automated form submissions, and malicious bots).
  • Language Preference: Handled via a functional technical cookie (NEXT_LOCALE) and HTTP request headers to display content in English or Arabic.
  • Request Timestamps: Exact date and time of web requests and form transmissions.

C. What We Do Not Collect on This Site

Our corporate website is strictly an informational and lead-inquiry platform. We do not process online credit card payments, handle consumer subscriptions, or store financial billing data. Furthermore, we do not request or process sensitive personal data, such as biometric identifiers, medical data, or political beliefs.

3. How and Why We Use Information

We process collected data exclusively for targeted, legitimate business purposes:

  • Inquiry Assessment and Response: Reviewing project inquiries, evaluating operational feasibility, and conducting preliminary scoping discussions.
  • Commercial Proposal Delivery: Preparing tailored business proposals, statements of work, and enterprise architectural recommendations.
  • Platform Security and Integrity: Monitoring and mitigating spam, brute-force attacks, security vulnerabilities, and abusive traffic patterns.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Fulfilling applicable legal, tax, accounting, and documentation duties.

Applicable Legal Bases: In accordance with international data protection frameworks, including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020, and the GDPR, our processing relies on:

  • Legitimate Business Interests: Responding to voluntary commercial communications and securing our digital infrastructure.
  • Pre-Contractual Measures: Evaluating technical requirements and taking preliminary steps at the data subject’s express request prior to entering into a commercial agreement.
  • Legal Compliance: Satisfying mandatory statutory obligations and regulatory directives.

Zero AI Training on Inquiries: Personal data submitted through our website contact form is never used to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or develop artificial intelligence models, machine learning algorithms, or LLMs. We do not perform automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or significant consequences for visitors.

4. Service Providers and Data Sharing

EVEXA does not sell, rent, lease, trade, or commercially monetize personal data under any circumstances. We share information only under strict operational boundaries:

  • Hosting and Infrastructure Providers: Technical data and contact submissions are stored on secure servers operated by contracted infrastructure and hosting vendors bound by confidentiality covenants and data protection agreements. (Note: Formal host provider entity and physical region subject to infrastructure verification).
  • Legal and Regulatory Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, enforceable court summons, subpoena, or lawful request by competent governmental authorities.
  • Corporate Reorganization: In the event of a merger, acquisition, corporate restructuring, or transfer of assets, prospective client correspondence may be transferred to the successor entity under equivalent privacy standards.

5. Cookies and Website Analytics

We adhere to a privacy-first approach regarding browser storage technologies:

  • No Advertising or Marketing Trackers: This website does not use advertising cookies, remarketing pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling trackers. We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or commercial ad networks.
  • Strictly Functional Language Cookie: The website utilizes a single technical first-party cookie named NEXT_LOCALE. Its exclusive role is to remember your language preference (en or ar) as you browse across pages. This cookie does not track personal browsing habits or link to personal identities.
  • Browser Controls: You may configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time. Because our single cookie is purely functional for language selection, disabling it will not impair website access, though you may need to re-select your preferred language upon subsequent visits.

6. Data Retention and Security

Technical Safeguards: We apply industry-standard technical and organizational security controls to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Transport Encryption: All website traffic is encrypted in transit using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Schema Validation: Submissions are validated server-side using strict schema verification (via Zod) to reject malformed data, script injection, and payload overflow.
  • Restricted Access & Rate Limiting: Stored contact records are accessible only to designated personnel. Server endpoints are protected by in-memory rate limiting to deter automated abuse.

Retention Guidelines: Inquiries are retained for the duration necessary to evaluate and address your commercial request, maintain professional correspondence history, or fulfill statutory accounting and recordkeeping requirements. (Note: Specific corporate document retention timelines subject to final operational confirmation).

While we implement rigorous security measures, please note that no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely impenetrable.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law [PDPL], the Egyptian Law No. 151/2020, and the GDPR), you may exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access / Knowledge: Request confirmation of whether we hold your personal data and obtain a readable copy of that information.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the correction, updating, or completion of any inaccurate or outdated records.
  • Right to Erasure / Destruction: Request the deletion or destruction of your personal data when the original purpose of processing has ended, subject to statutory retention mandates.
  • Right to Restriction / Objection: Request the restriction of processing or object to processing based on legitimate business interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is predicated on voluntary consent, withdraw that consent at any time without retroactively invalidating prior lawful processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy desk at support@evexa.tech. We will verify your identity before processing and respond within statutory timeframes.

8. Children’s Privacy and Policy Updates

Children’s Privacy: The EVEXA website is dedicated exclusively to enterprise, commercial, and professional audiences. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from individuals under 18 years of age (or the applicable age of legal majority). If we learn that a minor has submitted personal information through our contact form, we will take immediate measures to permanently delete that record.

Policy Modifications: We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our technical systems, operational modifications, or changes in applicable laws. Any revisions will be published directly on this page, with the policy status at the top updated accordingly. We encourage periodic review to remain informed of our data protection standards.

9. Contact and Privacy Requests

If you have questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or EVEXA’s data protection practices, please contact our team:

  • Privacy Contact: support@evexa.tech
  • Corporate Controller Details: Official registered commercial entity name, company registration number, and corporate office address are maintained in corporate records and provided during formal business contracting.

Supervisory Authorities: If you believe your personal data concerns have not been satisfactorily resolved, you have the right to lodge an inquiry or complaint with the competent supervisory authority in your jurisdiction (such as the Saudi Data & AI Authority [SDAIA] in Saudi Arabia, the Data Protection Centre in Egypt, or the relevant data protection authority in your country of residence).
